# Evaluation methodology — draft 0.1

## Unit of evaluation
A claim about a named product/service, plan, version, jurisdiction, and observation date. Platform-wide generalizations require evidence across relevant plans and regions.

## Evidence classes
A: reproducible direct test or primary technical documentation. B: dated official policy/documentation or regulatory filing. C: credible independent research. D: attributable user report or secondary coverage. U: unknown/not checked. Evidence class is not a truth guarantee.

## Dimensions
Score exportability, interoperability, identity portability, governance, operational independence, switching cost, accessibility, and security/privacy transparency independently. Use 0–4 only when evidence supports a judgment; use null for unknown. Explain scores and counter-evidence. Do not publish a composite ranking until weights, normalization, and sensitivity analysis are reviewed.

## Anti-bias controls
Disclose conflicts; do not accept payment for ranking; include platform responses; publish corrections; record version/date; distinguish product limitation from intent.
